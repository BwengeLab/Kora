# Organization Owner (CEO / Founder / MD) — UI/UX, Navigation & Features

*Experience name: "Business Command Center." The ultimate authority — sees the whole business, approves the biggest items, owns the account. Entry on sign-in → **Home**.*

---

## Navigation (this role's sidebar)
Home · Reconciliation · Action Center · Ledger & Cashflow · Collections · Reports · Value/ROI · Relationships · Contracts · Credit Passport · AI Agents · Audit & Risk · Consent · Settings · + copilot, search, notifications. *(Full access — overview-weighted.)*

---

## Pages / screens

### Home — Business Command Center
- **Purpose:** the whole business at a glance + what needs the owner.
- **On it:** Cash position · Revenue · Receivables · Payables (KPI cards w/ trends) · **Cash Flow** chart · **AI Insights** (forecast, overdue, rising expense, margin pressure) · **Top approvals awaiting you** (high-value/high-risk only) · Reconciliation snapshot · External Relationships summary · **Credit Passport** score · **ROI/value** · AI Agent activity · recent documents.
- **Features/functions:** drill anywhere; **executive-summary generator** ("summarize this week"); date-range; copilot.

### Action Center (top-tier approvals)
- **Purpose:** approve the highest-value/high-risk items routed up.
- **On it:** the owner's approval queue (big items, dual-approval, escalations), full evidence, risk badges.
- **Features:** approve/reject/escalate; SoD guard; can act as the second approver on over-limit items.

### Reports · Value/ROI
- **Purpose:** see performance & Kora's value for strategic decisions.
- **On it:** CEO/board reports, ROI dashboard — all evidence-backed, exportable.

### Ledger & Cashflow · Reconciliation · Collections · Relationships · Contracts · AI Agents · Audit · Consent
- **Purpose:** full visibility to drill into any area when needed (not the daily focus, but always available).
- **On it:** the same modules other roles use, in read/act mode per the owner's full authority.

### Credit Passport
- **Purpose:** the business's own credit standing; decide external sharing.
- **On it:** score, trends, affordability, evidence; **share/revoke** with lenders (consent).

### Settings
- **Purpose:** owner oversight of org config (often delegated to Admin).
- **On it:** org profile, users/roles, policies, integrations, billing, data — viewable/manageable.

---

## Navigation / workflow flow (a typical check-in)
1. Sign in → **Home (Business Command Center)** → scan health, AI insights, top approvals.
2. **Action Center** → approve the big/high-risk items only.
3. **Reports / ROI** → review performance + Kora's value; generate a board summary.
4. **Credit Passport / Consent** → manage who can see the business's financials.
5. Drill into any area as needed; use copilot for "show me X."
> Receives escalations from **Finance Lead**; delegates day-to-day to Finance Lead/Operator and config to **Admin**.

## Features & functions summary
View everything · approve top/high-risk items (incl. 2nd approver) · executive summaries · generate board reports · view ROI · share/revoke Credit Passport · oversee settings. The owner's experience is **breadth + decision**, not daily data entry.
